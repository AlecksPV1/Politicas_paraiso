## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the heritage, prestige, and quiet luxury of an exclusive country club lifestyle, translating it into a crisp, high-performing digital companion. The user experience is tailored to distinguished club members, golf enthusiasts, and tournament players who demand effortless utility alongside uncompromising aesthetic grace. 

The visual style blends **Modern Organic Luxury** with **Refined Minimalism**:
- **Tactile Warmth & Serenity:** Rather than cold tech-oriented stark whites, the interface is rooted in creamy alabaster, warm bone, and rich deep fairway greens. 
- **Subtle Sport Prestige:** Energetic fairway accents (vibrant lime/olive tones drawn from the club's trademark emblem) are used purposefully for real-time betting states, live hole indicators, and tee-time confirmations without breaking elegance.
- **Editorial Legibility:** Editorial typography pairings evoke clubhouse traditions and classic scorecards while retaining fluid touch ergonomics for fast in-round scoring, balance tracking, and peer settlements.
- **Understated Restraint:** Elements breathe through generous whitespace, bespoke hairline separators, and calm micro-interactions reminiscent of fine stationery.

## Colors

The color system is organized around the natural palette of championship golf courses: morning greens, sand traps, and sunlit greenswards.

- **Primary (`#1B4D3E` - Deep Fairway Green):** Represents authority, heritage, and focus. Used for primary navigation bars, main action surfaces, hero stat blocks, and authoritative headers. Paired with `fairway-deep` (`#123C2F`) for pressed interactive states and deep canvas footers.
- **Secondary (`#8BAE23` - Olive / Golf Lime):** Direct translation of the club logo's vibrant core. Serves as the primary active marker, selected segment indicator, handicap progress indicator, and active bet or tee-time badge.
- **Tertiary (`#C5A869` / `luxury-gold` `#B3924C`):** Muted satin gold used strictly for celebratory moments, tournament trophies, VIP/Honors designations, and high-stakes financial totals.
- **Neutral Surface Palette:**
  - `surface-cream` (`#FDFBF7`): The default canvas background, softening glare during bright outdoor rounds.
  - `neutral` (`#F4EFE6`): Secondary card containers and scorecard cell fills.
  - `sand-light` (`#E8DFD0`) & `sand-border` (`#D8CDBD`): Natural dividers, input outlines, and unselected status bars.
- **Contrast Text Inks:** `ink-primary` (`#1A2421`) delivers WCAG AAA contrast without the harsh artificial look of pure black; `ink-secondary` (`#53635B`) provides clear hierarchy for secondary metrics, labels, and timestamps.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy balances timeless prestige with high-stress digital legibility.

- **Display & Section Titles (Playfair Display):** Evoking high-end country club tradition, heritage editorial publications, and timeless luxury. Applied strictly to top-level page greetings, tournament trophy headers, and prestige banners.
- **Interface & Operational Text (Plus Jakarta Sans):** A precise, contemporary geometric sans-serif featuring crisp apertures and balanced proportions. Ensures instantaneous data comprehension on scorecards, wager leaderboards, tee-time booking selectors, and financial balance tables under bright sunlight.
- **Eyebrow & Sub-headers:** Displayed in uppercase `label-eyebrow` with wide letter-spacing (`0.08em`), bringing editorial polish to card headers, hole categories, and balance classifications.
- **Score & Financial Numerics:** Numerical values utilize `label-numeric` with proportional lining digits for structured column alignment across betting matrices, stableford scores, and statements.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout language is governed by calm proportion, disciplined grid alignment, and expansive vertical breathing room.

- **Grid Architecture:**
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid layout with `1rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Touch targets for golf operations maintain a minimum physical target size of 48px to accommodate one-handed golf cart usage.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8-column layout with `2rem` margins. Ideal for dual-pane tournament scorekeeping and tee-sheet administration.
  - **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column layout maxing out at `1280px` centered canvas, with `3rem` margins to preserve an intimate, editorial feel without overly wide data rows.
- **Rhythm & Padding:**
  - Component spacing relies on strict multiples of 4px/8px.
  - Section blocks utilize `space-xl` and `space-2xl` to separate financial balances, betting slips, and schedule reservations, preventing the interface from feeling cluttered or overwhelming.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects intense artificial dropshadows in favor of **Tonal Layering** and **Sunlit Ambient Halos**:

- **Surface Tiers:**
  - **Base Canvas:** `#FDFBF7` (Surface Cream).
  - **Tier 1 (Resting Cards & Lists):** `#FFFFFF` paired with an ultra-soft border (`1px solid #E8DFD0`) and a diffused warm ambient shadow: `0px 4px 20px rgba(27, 77, 62, 0.04)`.
  - **Tier 2 (Interactive Floating Modules, Dropdowns, Date Pickers):** `#FFFFFF` with shadow `0px 10px 30px rgba(27, 77, 62, 0.08)` and border `1px solid #D8CDBD`.
  - **Tier 3 (Modal Sheets, Betting Slips, Score Input Overlays):** Background `#FFFFFF` or `#123C2F` (for night/clubhouse mode) elevated by `0px 24px 48px rgba(18, 60, 47, 0.16)`.
- **Prestige Accents:** Subtle hairline borders (1px) in `sand-border` (`#D8CDBD`) provide structural distinction without relying on heavy shadows, giving every card the tactile crispness of an engraved member card.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts Level `2` (Rounded) geometry, achieving the ideal balance between structural elegance and friendly modern mobility.

- **Standard Elements (0.5rem / 8px):** Input text fields, table cells, score matrix chips, and inline actionable controls.
- **Containers & Cards (1rem / 16px):** Member wallet cards, upcoming reservation panels, betting pools, and round overview modules.
- **Modals & Drawers (1.5rem / 24px):** Bottom scoring sheets, bet settlement drawers, and confirmation modals.
- **Pill Exceptions (Full Radius / 9999px):** Status chips, handicap tags, filter pills, and circular hole-number markers to emulate the silhouette of a golf ball.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Brand Green):** Solid `#1B4D3E` fill with pure white typography, `border-radius: 8px`, 14px uppercase/semi-bold lettering with slight letter spacing. Hover/active shifts to `#123C2F`.
- **Secondary Action (Subtle Border):** `#FFFFFF` background with `1px solid #1B4D3E` border and `#1B4D3E` text.
- **Accent/Betting Action:** `#8BAE23` fill with `#123C2F` high-contrast bold typography for live bet confirmations, skin additions, and "Book Tee Time" highlights.
- **Ghost/Tertiary:** No border, transparent background, `#1B4D3E` or `#53635B` text with an underline animation on hover.

### Cards & Member Panels
- **Member Overview & Balance Card:** Rich container styled in `#1B4D3E` with subtle muted gold (`#C5A869`) accents for the balance figure, white display labels, and an embossed golf ball pattern outline in faint opacity (5%).
- **Round / Tee-Time Card:** Clean `#FFFFFF` container with `1px solid #E8DFD0`, featuring an 8px vertical accent bar on the left edge denoting round status (confirmed, in-play, or completed).

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Chips:** Full pill radius (`rounded-full`), padded `4px 12px`.
  - *Active Bet / In-Play:* `#F2F7E6` background, `#1B4D3E` text, and a `#8BAE23` dot indicator.
  - *Handicap Badge:* `#F4EFE6` fill, `#1A2421` text, `1px solid #D8CDBD`.
  - *VIP / Gold Honor:* `#FAF6ED` fill, `#B3924C` text, `1px solid #E2D3B3`.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Form Inputs:** Resting state in `#FFFFFF` with `1px solid #D8CDBD` borders, `0.5rem` radius, and `12px 16px` padding. Focus state features a crisp `#1B4D3E` outline and a delicate ambient glow (`0 0 0 3px rgba(27, 77, 62, 0.1)`). Floating labels utilize `label-eyebrow` formatting.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Soft-rounded squares for checkboxes and circles for radios with a `#1B4D3E` checked fill and custom white check/dot. Unchecked states use `1.5px solid #D8CDBD`.

### Lists & Sports Tables
- **Scorecards & Bet Tracking:** Alternating rows using `#FFFFFF` and `#FDFBF7`. Hairline dividers in `#E8DFD0`. Numeric cells for golf strokes (Eagles, Birdies, Pars, Bogeys) are centered with standard color-coded badges (Birdie: double circle in `#8BAE23`; Bogey: square in `#B94A48`).
- **Partner Balance List:** Member avatar on the left, name and club ID in primary ink, net financial status right-aligned (Positive green `#1B4D3E` with prefix `+`, Negative `#B94A48` with prefix `-`).

### Specialized Golf & Club Components
- **Hole Selector Carousel:** Horizontal snap carousel featuring circular pills containing hole numbers 1 through 18, highlighting current active holes with a glowing `#8BAE23` ring.
- **Wager Ledger:** Quick-add wager chips (e.g., "$100", "$250", "$500", "Nassau", "Skins") utilizing outlined neutral pills that fill with `#1B4D3E` when toggled.
